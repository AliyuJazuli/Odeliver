# ODeliver

### Offline-First Sales and Delivery Management Application

ODeliver is an Android application designed to simplify sales, inventory, and delivery management for small retailers and wholesalers.

The application provides a structured way to manage products, record sales, monitor inventory, and track deliveries through an offline-first approach. It is designed to remain functional without a constant internet connection, making it suitable for businesses operating in areas with unreliable connectivity.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Small retail businesses often rely on manual processes to manage their daily operations. ODeliver aims to improve these workflows by bringing essential business management tools into one application.

The application focuses on simplicity, reliability, and efficient business operations.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Sales Management:** Record and manage customer transactions.
- **Inventory Management:** Keep track of products and available stock.
- **Product Management:** Organize and manage business products.
- **Delivery Management:** Manage customer orders and delivery operations.
- **Offline-First Architecture:** Access essential application functionality without an internet connection.
- **Local Data Storage:** Store business data locally using Room Database.
- **Data Consistency:** Maintain reliable records across business operations.
